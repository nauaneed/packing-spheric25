Summary


Presented a packing proceedure with the following desirable features:

  1. ✅ Simultaneous initialization of fluid and solid
  2. ✅ Adaptive resolution
  3. ✅ Avoids complex procedures
  4. ✅ Efficient and fast
  5. ✅ Uses common SPH building blocks
  6. ✅ Open source: gitlab.com/pypr/particle-packing (public soon)
